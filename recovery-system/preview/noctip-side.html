Hello, we are evaluating this exact pillow for customers in Spain.
Please quote 2 samples delivered to Spain, including shipping and taxes.

Please confirm:
1. Exact model and a real video of the product and adjustment mechanism.
2. Is the height changed by a removable insert, different variants or another method?
3. Exact dimensions, weight, packed carton dimensions and cover/filling composition.
4. Washing instructions and the documents available for this exact foam and cover.
5. Spain/EU warehouse location, stock on hand, carrier and realistic delivery time.
6. Sample price delivered; MOQ and landed price at 50, 100 and 500 pieces.
7. Custom label/packaging MOQ and defect/replacement policy.

Please send the answers and documents through the platform chat.
